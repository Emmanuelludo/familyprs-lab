MODEL SET

Statistical models and nonlinear alternatives

Every model uses the same prediction horizon and family-held-out test set. The three PGS are entered jointly where appropriate.

Multi-PGS genetic block

P+T, lassosum and LDpred2 provide correlated views of the same underlying genetic signal. A strongly regularized stack prevents redundant scores from dominating.

Elastic Net

Three PGS, clinical covariates and explicit family-history variables. Shrinkage handles correlation among scores and related predictors.

Largest standardized coefficients

Family-aware estimation

The random-intercept model estimates residual family clustering directly. GEE provides a population-average clustered analysis. A shared gamma-frailty model treats time to IBD onset explicitly.

AutoML benchmark

XGBoost, LightGBM and CatBoost are tuned with grouped development CV. They test whether nonlinear thresholds and interactions improve transportable prediction.

SENSITIVITY

Robustness to imperfect measurement

The fitted models are perturbed with noisier PGS measurements, missing family history, exposure misclassification and outcome error.

Model sensitivity

Data-generating parameters

Shared gamma frailty induces residual family dependence in the prospective outcome. The event process uses a ten-year exponential baseline hazard with evidence-informed relative effects.

METHODS & DATA

Evidence, simulation and modelling are kept separate

The genetic evidence comes from public IBD PGS resources; the cohort generator supplies the family structure needed for method development.

Data provenance

One acknowledgement for the demonstrator: the PGS definitions and published performance summaries are public; participant-level pedigrees, clinical covariates and outcomes are simulated. No UKSH participant-level data are used.

Data-generating model
